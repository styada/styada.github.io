# Introduction

With the popularity of ChatGPT and LLMs reaching new heights, our world is changing in ways we never imagined. 
Before generative AI spread like wildfire into households everywhere, most non-techy folks thought of artificial 
intelligence as either self-driving cars—the poster child of AI back then—or maybe a Boston Dynamics robot demo 
they stumbled upon on YouTube. Sometimes, that curiosity led to watching the 2004 "I, Robot" movie with Will Smith 
and wondering if robots might one day rise up and take over.

Back then, the general sentiment in the tech industry—and among the public—was that AI was just a bunch of "if-else" 
statements strung together. But for all the speculation, one thing was clear to everyone: it wasn’t a matter of "if," 
but "when" AI would become a common part of our lives and homes. That prediction has come true, at least in part. 
If you have a device that connects to Wi-Fi, you already have access to some of the world’s most complex models, 
with hundreds of billions of parameters.

Even so, while AI chatbots have become popular and undeniably useful, most people still don’t understand them well 
and remain skeptical. On top of that, the pace of industry-shifting developments is dizzying—every few months, 
something new and important emerges, making it tough for anyone to keep up. Having worked full-time in GenAI for a 
couple of years now, I’ve noticed just how little most people know about the depths of this rabbit hole we call GenAI.

That’s why I’m putting together a series of blog posts, hand-crafted for the average joe, to help you start 
understanding this technology—without any barriers to entry. My goal isn’t to write a textbook or bombard you with 
 formulas, but to have a clear, honest discussion.
