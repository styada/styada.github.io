Tech conferences, the assemblies of some of the most brilliant engineers, decision makers, and ideators in the industry all in one spot.
While going through college, I always wanted to attend prestigious tech conferences like AI4 or Nvidia's GTC or World Summit AI.
But, very quickly I realized that these conferences on a college student budget are extremely cost prohibitive especially if the conference happened to involve travel to another city.
So I was very glad that there was a tech conference happening right in my "backyard" in the Minneapolis-St. Paul area. 
Even more so I was elated to find out that I was going to be sent as a representative of my company to capture and understand the current discussions being had in the 
community and to see if some of them can be implemented at home base on my team. 

With the intro aside, what exactly did I find out and learn?

My focus for the day given my background and current work was on all things AI. I wanted to find out what bets people are placing on AI and how they are shaping their 
systems and processes to capitalize on the hype train.
